## Rules (from the SEO map post and Claude's/Google's policies)
- Answer the question in the first sentence of every page.
- Each page says something no other page says (real facts, real templates), never the same text with a word swapped.
- Fact-check AI-written text by hand; no fake reviews, fake personas or "citation seeding" on sites we don't own.
- Earn real mentions (directories, Product Hunt) with the same description everywhere.
